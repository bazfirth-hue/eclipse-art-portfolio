Commissions

How a Piece Gets Made

Every commission runs the same four steps, so you always know where your piece is up to.

  1. 01

    Tell me the idea

    Subject, size, colours, where it is going to hang. Send references if you have them, a rough description is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Sketch and quote

    I come back with the approach and a fixed price. Nothing is sprayed and nothing is owed until you are happy with both.

  3. 03

    Painted by hand

    Built up in layers with spray cans, no printing and no digital work at any stage. You get progress photos as it comes together.

  4. 04

    Sealed and shipped

    Varnished to protect the surface, packed hard-cornered and boxed, then shipped tracked anywhere in the world.

Typical turnaround is 4 to 6 weeks from the deposit, depending on size and how many pieces are already in the queue.
