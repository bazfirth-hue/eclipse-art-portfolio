Commissions

How a Piece Gets Made

Commissions are space scenes only: planets, nebulae, eclipses and alien cities, sprayed in the colours you choose. Every one runs the same four steps, so you always know where your piece is up to.

  1. 01

    Tell me the idea

    Which planets, what colours, how big, where it is going to hang. Send references if you have them, a rough description is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Sketch and quote

    I come back with the approach and a fixed price. Nothing is sprayed and nothing is owed until you are happy with both.

  3. 03

    Painted by hand

    Built up in layers with spray cans, no printing and no digital work at any stage. You get progress photos as it comes together.

  4. 04

    Sealed and shipped

    Varnished to protect the surface, packed hard-cornered and boxed, then posted to your door anywhere in the UK.

Typical turnaround is 4 to 6 weeks from the deposit, depending on size and how many pieces are already in the queue.

Good to Know

Questions Before You Buy

What is the difference between an original and a print?

The original is the actual painting James sprayed by hand, and only one exists. It is £200. A print is a reproduction of that painting for £25, so you can own the image at a fraction of the price, even after the original has sold.

Is delivery included?

Yes. Prices include delivery anywhere in the UK, so the price you see is the price you pay. For now James only posts within the UK. If you are overseas, get in touch and ask.

What if it is not right for me?

You have 14 days from delivery to change your mind. Get in touch, send it back in the condition it arrived, and you will be refunded.

How is the artwork made?

Every original is built up by hand in layers of spray paint. There is no printing and no digital work in an original at any stage.

Can I have something made just for me?

Yes, as long as it is a space scene. Planets, nebulae, eclipses and alien cities, in the colours you want. Tell James the idea and he will come back with a sketch and a fixed price before anything is sprayed. See how commissions work.
